2 Build order

F1 · Foundations (2–3 weeks)

  • Agent registry: REN tag no., expiry, PI insurance, level
  • Written agency agreement (commission 40% + Marketing Bonus wording)
  • Upline tree + titles (referral record kept separate)

F2 · Money engine (3–4 weeks)

  • Commission + Marketing Bonus calculator with the 40% hard cap
  • Two-voucher output, self-billed e-invoice fields
  • Claim pipeline with approval checkpoint

F3 · Documents (3–4 weeks)

  • Form chain with shared reference numbers
  • E-signature for both parties
  • Milestone release tied to documents

F4 · Performance & compliance (2–3 weeks)

  • Rank progress, leaderboard, campaigns
  • Monthly report: declared commission vs total paid out
  • REN tag and insurance reminders

Deliberately left out

Atlas carries modules we do not need at this size: market radar, auctions, valuations, an internal store, and a global agent network. Adding them would slow F1–F2 and add compliance surface. They can be revisited once the agency passes ~40 agents.
