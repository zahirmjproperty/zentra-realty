3 How an override is triggered

Rule set (config)

  • Override is paid on closed deals only — never on recruitment, never on a joining fee.
  • Override comes out of the agency share, is labeled Marketing Bonus, and posts to its own account code (602-150) — never merged into the declared commission line (602-100).
  • Levels are earned on production (closed production, rolling 12 months) — exactly as in the ladder: L1 REN tag + onboarding · L2 RM600k · L3 RM1.5m · L4 RM2.5m + 2 mentored agents · L5 RM3.5m + 3 producing agents · L6 RM6m + 2 team leaders.
  • Qualification: a leader only earns override on a member in the month the member completes a deal, and only while the leader keeps 3 personally-closed deals per year.
  • Referrer ≠ leader: the referral record (who introduced whom) carries no payment right. Override runs on the management upline only, and only to registered, active agents (Act 242).
  • Rate versioning: rates are locked to the version in force when the agreement was signed (v1.0, effective 01 Jan 2026). A ladder change applies to new agreements only — it never rewrites what an existing agent already signed.
  • Cap + clawback: override stops at the annual cap; if a deal falls through after payout, the engine raises a clawback against the next run.

Worked example

Negotiator Aisy (level 60%) closes a subsale with a fee of RM10,000. The chain that pays is her management upline (who supervises her, not who introduced her): Faizal Omar (L4 · 75%) → Nadia Rahman (L6 · 90%, override capped at 10%). A referrer who is not her leader is kept as a record and is paid RM0.
