Scheduled chasers

Signing reminders go out on day 1, 3 and 5. The agency sees the same list, so nobody has to remember who to call.

Hard deadlines

The stamping clock is a legal deadline: 30 days from the last signature. Alerts at 14, 7 and 2 days left, then daily.

Money events

Claim submitted → approved → invoiced → released. Every step notifies the negotiator and the leader who supervises the deal.
