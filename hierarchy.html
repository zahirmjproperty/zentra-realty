3 Promotion rules (and the anti-pyramid line)

Promotion is earned, not bought

  • ✓
    No joining fee, no kit fee, no stock purchase. Nothing an agent pays to enter.
  • ✓
    Qualification counts closed production, not headcount of recruits.
  • ✓
    Coaching requirement: L5 needs 3 producing agents — agents who themselves closed deals, verified by settlement records.
  • ✓
    Override stops if the leader stops leading: two consecutive quarters below the coaching minimum → override suspended, level reviewed.
  • ✓
    One chain only. Override flows up two levels maximum, never deeper (that is where a lawful incentive plan starts to look like a chain scheme).

Red flags the system blocks

  • ✕
    Reward computed mainly from recruitment rather than closed deals
  • ✕
    Any entry payment, deposit, or "level purchase" by an agent
  • ✕
    Payout with no matching property transaction in the trail
  • ✕
    Commission line above 40% of the professional fee
  • ✕
    Override paid to someone who is not a registered negotiator or agency staff member
Why: Malaysian law treats a scheme whose income comes mainly from recruitment as an unlawful pyramid/chain scheme, even when it also sells something. Keeping every ringgit tied to a real transaction is the design rule.
