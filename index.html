Zentra Realty — Overview

Agent management for Zentra Property Group, built around the IQI payout structure: a declared negotiator commission capped at 40% of the professional fee, plus a Marketing Bonus paid out of the agency share for level and leader incentives.

2 How a payout splits

Sample: rental at Seri Harmoni, Kajang · professional fee RM1,018.52 · negotiator level 85% · leader 15% — the exact shape of the vouchers issued on 30 Jun 2026.

85%to negotiator
Commission 40%
Bonus 45%
Leader 15%
Declared commission — RM407.41 Marketing Bonus (agent) — RM458.33 Marketing Bonus (leader) — RM152.78
LineAmount% of fee
Negotiator's commission declaredRM407.4140.0%
Marketing Bonus — agent levelRM458.3345.0%
Total to negotiatorRM865.7485.0%
Marketing Bonus — leader overrideRM152.7815.0%
Reconciles to the professional feeRM1,018.52100.0%

Rounding: RM407.41 + RM458.33 + RM152.78 = RM1,018.52 exactly. Any odd sen is absorbed by the agency residual, never silently folded into a payee line.

Two separate documents are produced: the commission voucher (never above 40%) and the Marketing Bonus voucher. They post to separate account codes — the capped commission to 602-100, the Marketing Bonus to 602-150 — so a regulator can see which line was capped and which was funded by the agency.

Why two documents?

  • ✓
    Only 40% is called commission. The declared line stays inside the ceiling recognised in practice for negotiators.
  • ✓
    Everything above it is agency-funded. Level bonus and leader override come out of the agency share, labelled Marketing Bonus.
  • ✓
    Transaction-based. Every ringgit traces to a real deal — fee, invoice, voucher, unit reference.
  • ✓
    Audit-ready. The system prints "declared commission vs total paid" every month, so nothing is discovered late.
Internal note (pending legal review). The negotiator’s declared commission stays within the ceiling; the level reward and the leader share are paid by the agency from its own share as a marketing reward on a completed transaction, on separate documents and with a full money trail. Whether a regulator would accept that separation is a question for our lawyers — the control panel lets the agency switch the whole engine to a single-line payout inside the ceiling if the answer is no.
