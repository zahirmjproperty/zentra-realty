Agent onboarding

Six steps from “interested” to “earning”. The system will not release a commission until the registration, the insurance and the bank details are in — so nothing is discovered at payout time.

WORKING DEMO · SAMPLE DATA

1 Why each step exists

Registration

A negotiator works under a registered estate agency. No registration, no commission claim — there is no way around this in law.

Insurance & agreement

Professional indemnity cover, the negotiator agreement (signed electronically) and the PDPA consent are needed before the first letter goes out.

Bank, TIN & e-invoice

Payouts are self-billed e-invoices. Wrong bank or missing TIN means the payout run stops on release day.
